Met deze motivatiebrief wil ik graag solliciteren naar de positie van Adviseur Crisisbeheersing Drinkwater bij Waternet. De werkzaamheden en doelstellingen van deze functie spreken mij bijzonder aan. 
Hoewel ik inmiddels bijna twee jaar in Nederland woon, ben ik de taal nog aan het leren en druk ik mijzelf makkelijker uit in het Engels:

\vspace{0.7em}

I would like to apply for the advisor position for crisis management. The position raised my interest because it combines my passion for water quality management and and my desire to educate and help people. I am keen on drafting scenarios and plans 
to prepare for potential crises. Therefore, want to help Waternet to supply the citizens of Amsterdam with safe drinking water. I want to assist in creating awareness and understanding of the importance of water quality. 

\vspace{0.7em}

During my first internship, I worked for the municipal water quality laboratory in Fürth, Germany. It was then that I learnt just how crucial every part of the water supply chain is. To prevent contamination, 
it is important to always stay one step ahead of a potential crisis. During my internship, I was involved in the daily monitoring of water quality, which included not only checking taps but also taking samples from reservoirs and wells. 
That was a first step towards understanding how closely everything is interconnected -- particularly when it comes to water quality. On a daily basis, I was driving around the city with my collegues to collect samples. I got a whole new perspective on 
the water supply system -- an insight that usually is locked away to the public.

\vspace{0.7em}

As part of my Bachelor's thesis, I completed a three-month internship with an austrian engineering company at a water treatment plant in Kikinda, Serbia. There, I gained practical insights into drinking water treatment and the importance of adhering to high water quality standards. 
What excited me most about this work was the problem-solving aspect. When there is a drop in pressure in the system or other parameters suddenly change, the cause of the problems must be identified. The fascination of not only solving 
these puzzles, but also working towards the solution step by step, motivated me every day. The resulting analysis of the situation showed me how important it is to understand what happened and to prevent it from happening again. 
Communication, teamwork and continued progress all help to strengthen a system. This fosters a certain readiness to deal with the uncertainty of similar events in the future. I am keen to apply my knowledge and learn how to share it with others. 
I want to help Waternet be prepared for the unexpected and ensure that the public can have confidence in the quality of their drinking water.
