Since before I started by studies, I have been interested in engineering. With my bachelor's in environmental engineering, I have found a love for the water sector. Planning projects, calculating and designing water distribution systems was a real pleasure. 
I want to apply my knowledge to projects, that have a direct impact on the quality of life of people. I know how important detailed planning is and how important the role of strong communicator can be. With my diverse background in engineering and also 
water management, I am confident that I can be the person necessary to keep a safe working space. 

\vspace{0.7em}

During my internship as part of my Bachelor's thesis in Kikinda, Serbia, I worked on the commissioning of a new drinking water treatment plant. In addition to the technical tasks -- evaluating parameters for coagulation, flocculation and filtration -- 
I took on project management responsibilities on site, which included ensuring a safe working environment for the on-site staff. This involved conducting safety briefings, monitoring the correct handling of chemicals such as 
hydrochloric acid and polyaluminium chloride, and ensuring that protocols were followed under conditions of actual operation. Whenever unexpected problems arose, such as pressure drops or parameter deviations, I learnt to systematically 
diagnose and coordinate efforts to resolve them. The top priority was always to ensure we could reliably supply the city with clean water. 

\vspace{0.7em}

My experience of coordinating safety aspects between technical specialists, staff from local utility companies and external project partners in Serbia 
have shown me that a clear safety plan depends on communication and the early involvement of the right people. It is not just a matter of documentation. 
What particularly appeals to me about Vitens is its move towards closer collaboration with contractors. Monitoring and assessing the work of contractors, coordinating with local authorities and other stakeholders, 
and maintaining quality standards in projects are the areas in which I wish to develop my skills further.
