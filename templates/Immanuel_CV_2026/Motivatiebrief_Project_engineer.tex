Met deze motivatiebrief wil ik graag solliciteren naar de positie van Project Engineer bij Dunea. De werkzaamheden en doelstellingen van deze functie spreken mij bijzonder aan. 
Hoewel ik inmiddels bijna twee jaar in Nederland woon, ben ik de taal nog aan het leren en druk ik mijzelf makkelijker uit in het Engels:

\vspace{0.7em}

Dunea played a direct role in bringing me to the Netherlands. During my Bachelor's in Environmental Engineering in Germany, I analysed the Freshman Project by Dunea. 
Evaluating that system introduced me to the Netherlands' approach to drinking water management. That work convinced me that the Netherlands, and the Dutch drinking water 
sector specifically, was where I would like to build my career. After I was told, even whilst I was doing my minor in Vienna, that the Netherlands would be perfect for me, 
I chose to pursue a Master's in Water Science and Management at Utrecht University, which I completed this year.

\vspace{0.7em}

My Bachelor's gave me practical engineering foundations directly relevant to this role. I designed water infrastructure for urban districts, calculated dimensions for both water treatment and wastewater treatment plants, 
worked with BIM for building design, and used AutoCAD for spatial planning and technical drawings across different scales. I am comfortable moving between designing, technical calculations, and on-site work.

\vspace{0.7em}

During my Bachelor's thesis internship in Kikinda, Serbia, I worked on the commissioning of a new EU-funded drinking water treatment plant. I was involved from technical preparation through to implementation. 
I was evaluating the performance of the treatment train, checking relevant physiochemical parameters, coordinating between disciplines, and holding on-site responsibility for safety. Whenever unexpected problems 
arose, such as pressure drops or parameter deviations, I learnt to systematically diagnose and coordinate efforts to resolve them. The top priority was always to ensure we could reliably supply the city with clean water. 

\vspace{0.7em}

What appeals to me about Dunea specifically is the complexity and multidisciplinary nature of the projects. From abstraction points to treatment infrastructure and the direct connection to drinking water quality. 
It is what I have been working towards for the last couple of years. Now I would like to apply my gathered knowledge and I think that Dunea is a great fit for me.
