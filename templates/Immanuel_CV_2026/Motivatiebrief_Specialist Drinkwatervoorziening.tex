Met deze motivatiebrief wil ik graag solliciteren naar de positie van Specialist Drinkwatervoorziening bij Oasen. De werkzaamheden en doelstellingen van deze functie spreken mij bijzonder aan. 
Hoewel ik inmiddels bijna twee jaar in Nederland woon, ben ik de taal nog aan het leren en druk ik mijzelf makkelijker uit in het Engels:

\vspace{0.7em}

Oasen supplies clean drinking water to many households in the Netherlands. As a water engineer, this is something I am passionate about. My academic background is built entirely around water: a Bachelor's in Environmental Engineering, 
where I focused on the design and hydraulic calculation of water distribution systems, followed by a Master's in Water Science and Management at Utrecht University, completed this year. 
I am now looking for a role where I can apply these skills in a practical setting, and I believe that Oasen is the perfect place to do so.

\vspace{0.7em}

During my internship as part of my Bachelor's thesis in Kikinda, Serbia, I worked on the commissioning of a new drinking water treatment plant. In addition to the technical tasks -- evaluating parameters for coagulation, flocculation and filtration -- 
I took on project management responsibilities on site, which included ensuring a safe working environment for the on-site staff. This involved conducting safety briefings, monitoring the correct handling of chemicals such as 
hydrochloric acid and polyaluminium chloride, and ensuring that protocols were followed under conditions of actual operation. Whenever unexpected problems arose, such as pressure drops or parameter deviations, I learnt to systematically 
diagnose and coordinate efforts to resolve them. The top priority was always to ensure we could reliably supply the city with clean water. Operational responibility is something I learned in Serbia and I can make the call myself when the 
situation requires it. 

\vspace{0.7em}

That internship also taught me that safety and coordination between technical specialists, local utility staff and external partners is not a documentation exercise -- it depends on clear communication and involving the right people early. 
It is not just a matter of documentation. Monitoring and assessing contractor work, coordinating with local authorities and other stakeholders, and holding the line on quality standards without compromising continuity of 
supply is what is what I want gain experience in. The position of a Specialist for Drinking Water Supply at Oasen is the perfect opportunity to do so.

\vspace{0.7em}

I understand this role includes participation in the fault service roughly once every six weeks, operational leadership duties within the watch service, and the flexibility to respond during calamities. I am ready to take these responsibilities on and 
am aware that a reliability of supply is most important. It is something that does not just stop at the end of a shift, but is a continuous responsibility.

\vspace{0.7em}

Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to hearing from you and discussing how I can contribute to Oasen's mission of providing safe and reliable drinking water.
